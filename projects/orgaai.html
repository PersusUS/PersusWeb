OrgaAI

I spent the summer of 2025 at OrgaAI working on a confidential state-of-the-art conversational AI programme. The work is covered by an NDA, so what follows is deliberately general — the specifics of the product, the models and the results are not mine to publish.

My focus was the audio processing and transcription pipeline: the stage that turns raw speech into something the rest of the system can reason over. Get it wrong and every downstream component inherits the mistake, which makes it an unglamorous but load-bearing part of any voice product.

The result I can point to is a throughput one. The pipeline was running full WhisperX transcription passes over material for which the dataset already contained transcripts — paying twice for information we already had. Restructuring the pipeline to use those existing transcripts strategically instead made it roughly ten times faster, with no loss of quality downstream. Most of that win came from questioning an assumption rather than from optimising code.

I also researched and prototyped a Slot Attention integration for architectural improvements. That strand stayed exploratory, and under the NDA it stays described exactly that way.
